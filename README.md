## 🔑 Key Features
1. **Time-Window Lock Mechanism**: Next day unlocks at **08:00 AM IST** and relocks at **08:00 PM IST** (with built-in IST Time Machine Sandbox and Override for review).
2. **Daily Pre & Post Assessments**: 5 MCQs before every session and 5 MCQs after, unlocking daily verified badges.
3. **Proctored Final Certification**: Day 5 proctored evaluation with webcam facial tracking, fullscreen check, and tab-switch violation audit.
4. **Official Kapil Certificate**: Downloadable & printable high-resolution certificate with unique verification hash and digital seal.
5. **Strict Google Authentication**: Integrated with official Firebase SDK (`placement-readiness-5-day`) with Google OAuth popup and single Demo User fallback.
6. **Dual Draggable Resizable Panels**: Dynamic drag handles on both left navigation and right mentorship drawer.
7. **White Background Theme**: Clean white palette with rainbow spectrum typography for headings.
