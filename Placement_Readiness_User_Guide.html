2.2 Step-by-Step Learner Execution Protocol

  • Authentication: Click Sign In with Google in the top header. Your cloud session ensures all progress, scores, and badges persist across devices.
  • Pre-Assessment: In the Day view, click Start Pre-Test. Submit your 10 answers. This records your baseline readiness score.
  • 5 Solved Practice Questions Masterclass:
    • Switch between C++, Java, Python, C, and HTML/JS tabs to inspect syntactic nuances.
    • Read Kapil's Intuition notes detailing edge-case traps (e.g., INT_MIN boundaries in median partitions).
    • Click Open in IDE to test code with custom inputs.
    • Click Mark as Complete for all 5 problems to satisfy progression criteria.
  • Solving Guided Problems: Tackle in-class problems directly in the integrated compiler or on HackerRank. Mark each problem as solved upon completion.
  • Earning Badges via Post-Assessment: Complete the 10 Post-Assessment questions. Scoring 7/10 or higher immediately awards the day's badge.
  • Daily Workshop Feedback (fpln.site):
    • Window: 2:30 PM to 3:30 PM IST daily.
    • Automated countdown banners notify learners at 2:00 PM (30 min reminder), 2:20 PM (10 min), and 2:25 PM (5 min).
    • Click Open Day Feedback to record your evaluation.
  • Unlocking Subsequent Days: When Day 1 is 100% complete, Day 2 unlocks automatically. If Day 1 remains incomplete, Day 2 displays the secure lock gate.

3.2 Core Administrative Operations & Workflows

A. Day Unlock & Relock Controller (Batch & Individual Management)

Instructor Kapil maintains absolute authority over content access. Days can be toggled globally or customized for individual learners.

  • Unlock All Days Globally: Bypasses all locks simultaneously for the entire cohort (ideal for weekend review sessions or open mock tests).
  • Relock All Days to Default: Restores strict chained gating (Day N requires Day N-1 completion).
  • Individual Student Remediation: Navigate to the student roster → Click Audit & Access → Toggle individual day overrides (FORCE UNLOCK or FORCE LOCK). Real-time events update the student's screen instantly without requiring page reloads.

B. Automated Placement Cell CSV Export Engine

College placement cells and training coordinators require clean, timestamped compliance data. The dashboard provides one-click CSV generation:

  • Daily Reports (Day 1 to 5): Exports student names, emails, UID, Pre-Test score, Post-Test score, exemplar count, and feedback status for that specific calendar day.
  • 5-Day Master Consolidated Report: Comprehensive multi-column spreadsheet containing total questions solved, all 5 pre/post scores, total badges won, and final certificate eligibility.

C. Credentials, Badges & Curriculum Extensions

Admin Kapil can issue official credentials and extend the curriculum directly from the portal:

  • Issue Custom Badges: Select student, select badge title (e.g. Graph Master, Dynamic Programming Wizard), add personalized commendation notes, and issue directly to their profile.
  • Issue Verified Certificates: Generate authenticated PDF-ready certificates for students achieving capstone milestones.
  • Deploy Assignments & Quizzes: Create new coding challenges with starter code and custom test cases, or author new multi-choice quizzes with detailed rationales.
